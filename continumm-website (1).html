Good work isn't the problem. The calendar is.

Most basement contractors we talk to lose their week to the same three things.

Shared leads turn into a race to the phone

Lead sites sell the same homeowner to three, four, sometimes five contractors. You're on a job site, you call back two hours later, and they've already booked someone. Whoever's cheapest wins.

Forty-five minutes to quote a $15k budget

An hour in the basement measuring, a proper quote written up at home, then: "Oh, we were thinking more like fifteen." Half a day gone on someone who was never going to spend $60k.

Are these shared leads?

No. Every appointment is exclusive to you and booked in your calendar. Nobody else gets that homeowner, and you only pay when they actually show up.

How is this different from HomeStars, Angi or Thumbtack?

Lead sites sell you a name and number, often shared with several contractors. We hand you a booked, qualified estimate visit that's yours alone, and you're only charged when the homeowner is there.

Basements take months to close. Does this work?

That's why we only book homeowners who want to start within 3 months, have accepted a realistic budget and will have every decision-maker at the visit.

What if the homeowner turns out to be a bad fit?

Flag it within 48 hours with the reason. If it broke our qualification rules, the appointment is credited.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee you only pay for homeowners who show up. Signing them is on you, but you'll be quoting serious people instead of price shoppers.

Why do you need a card on file?

So you're charged per result instead of paying a retainer up front. Each booking is charged automatically and credited in full if the homeowner doesn't show.

I've been burned by agencies before.

Most contractors have. That's exactly why there's no monthly retainer and no long contract. You pay per show, month to month, with 14 days' notice to stop.

How many appointments can I get?

We start at 5 to 6 shows in month one so the risk stays small, then 8 to 10 a month. Larger teams doing 3 to 6 basements a month can take 10 to 20.
